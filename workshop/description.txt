[h1]KnoxMap — real places, playable in Project Zomboid[/h1]

Draw a box around your town on a map and get a Build 42 map mod of it: the
real streets, the real buildings, furnished inside, with loot, zombies and
cars where they belong.

[b]KnoxMap is a program you run on your own PC, not a subscription.[/b] It is
free and open source, and it lives here:

[url=https://github.com/spytheeuclidean-a11y/knoxmap/releases/latest][h3]➜ Download KnoxMap from GitHub[/h3][/url]

Windows, Linux and macOS. Open the program file, draw an area, and work
through Setup, Map and Build. The map appears in your game as its own
starting region. KnoxMap reads the place from a Geofabrik extract on your PC.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]What this Workshop item is[/h2]

A small tool that comes with KnoxMap, put here on its own because it is
useful in any map — including the ones that ship with the game.

[b]Reset loot.[/b] Right-click the ground and pick [i]Reset loot[/i]: every
container in that building, or everything within 30 tiles, is filled again
from the game's own loot tables. No new save needed. Useful when you have
picked a town clean, when you install a map again, or when a map you are
building changes under you.

It asks before it does anything, and it never touches a vehicle, a corpse or
anyone's inventory — whatever is inside a container is thrown away first.

[b]It does not generate maps.[/b] That is KnoxMap itself, from the link above.

[h2]What KnoxMap makes[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]The real place.[/b] Streets, pavements, kerbs, road markings, rivers,
    coastline, woods, railways, bridges and overpasses, from OpenStreetMap.
[*] [b]Buildings that are buildings.[/b] Every footprint becomes a furnished
    one on its real outline: houses, blocks of flats, shops with their
    stockrooms, schools, churches, works, police stations, libraries, fire
    stations. A parade of shops becomes a parade, not one shed.
[*] [b]Rooms the game knows.[/b] Loot comes from Project Zomboid's own tables
    for each room, so a pharmacy is a pharmacy and a gun store is a gun store.
[*] [b]People, then zombies.[/b] How many lived and worked in each block is
    worked out from floor area and land use, and the spawn map is drawn from
    that — busy blocks are busy, and the streets are not empty.
[*] [b]Cars, petrol pumps, graveyards, army bases[/b], the paper map you open
    with M, and a spawn point in a house.
[/list]

[h2]What you need[/h2]

[list]
[*] Project Zomboid [b]Build 42[/b] — Build 41 cannot load these maps.
[*] The map tools and the tile artwork, which KnoxMap's own setup fetches and
    extracts from your copy of the game. Nothing is downloaded from anyone
    else's.
[*] [b]Windows, Linux or macOS.[/b] On Linux the map compiler comes built for
    Linux, so there is no Wine and nothing to install; on macOS the map tools
    run through Wine.
[*] Optional: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3780306632]Elevators[/url]
    for working lifts, [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3772052709]Spawn Selector[/url]
    to start at any landmark, and
    [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3346506593]Erika's Tiles[/url]
    for glass shop fronts and street signs.
[/list]
